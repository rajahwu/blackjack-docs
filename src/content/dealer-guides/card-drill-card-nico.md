### One‑Page Practice Drill Card — Victor "V" Cardwright

---

### Setup (1 minute)

- Deck: casino finish, no jokers.
- Surface: matte felt or non-reflective table.
- Camera: position so hands and table center frame; good overhead or 45° angle.
- Targets: mark two placement zones with tape (near player; far player).

---

### Warm‑up (3 minutes)

- Finger stretches: 30s thumb/index/middle each.
- Palm presses: 30s hold deck, feel friction.
- 10 shallow card slides (no deal) to warm thumb motion.

---

### Riffle Progression (15 minutes)

1. Single Riffle consistency — 8 min
    - Split decks evenly; riffle slowly; aim for full interleave.
    - Repeat 50 reps; target 40/50 clean interleaves.
2. Square + Bridge — 4 min
    - Riffle → press & square → bridge arc.
    - Repeat 20 reps; keep square within 2–3 mm.
3. Pressure control + Finish — 3 min
    - Alternate 10 light / 10 heavy riffles; practice one-handed finish 10 reps.

---

### Dealing Progression (15 minutes)

1. Grip & Slide — 5 min
    - Deal top card slide to palm; repeat 100 times; target 85/100 clean slides.
2. One‑Hand Deal sequences — 5 min
    - Deal 5 cards to each position, alternating sides; 20 sequences; aim for consistent orientation.
3. Placement Precision + Calls — 5 min
    - Deal to marked targets (50 deals); call totals aloud to metronome (80–100 BPM).

---

### Combined Flow Drills (10 minutes)

- Flow Loop x12
    - Riffle (single) → square → one-handed finish → deal 4-player hands (with calls) → clean square.
    - Target: 10/12 smooth loops, camera framed.
- Speed/Accuracy Sprint x2
    - Time 25 loops; keep ≥95% placement accuracy; try to improve time next session.

---

### Error Recovery Drill (5 minutes)

- Simulate misdeal/drop twice.
- Use studio script: “We will pause briefly while we review.”
- Practice supervisor call and calm reset; repeat until response is automatic.

---

### Recording & Review (5 minutes)

- Record a 60s clip of best riffles + 60s clip of a 5‑card deal round with calls.
- Quick playback: note one visual fix and one timing fix; log both.

---

### Daily Metrics (checklist)

- [] Riffle success rate (out of 50): ____
- [] Deal placement accuracy (out of 50): ____
- [] Flow loops clean (out of 12): ____
- [] Best 60s clip saved (Y/N): __

---

### Weekly Targets (end of week)

- Week 1: 40/50 riffles; 40/50 deals within target; 10/12 clean loops.
- Week 2: add reliable bridge; consistent one‑hand finishes.
- Week 3: double riffles and faster loops; 95% accuracy.
- Week 4: 5‑minute mock shift recorded and camera‑approved.

---

### Quick fixes (1 line each)

- Uneven interleave → widen initial separation slightly.
- Noisy cards → dry hands; swap deck stock.
- Misplaced deals → slow to target, then accelerate once consistent.
- Verbal timing off → practice with metronome.

---
