# 🂡 Card Drill — Nico

**Objective:** Build consistency in **dealing technique**, **hand rhythm**, and **table focus** under timed pressure.

---

## 🎴 Drill Overview

| Segment | Focus | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm-up shuffle | 2m | Gentle start, maintain even wrist rotation |
| 2 | 4-hand simulation | 5m | Announce totals, keep 1s cue gap |
| 3 | Split/Double simulation | 3m | Maintain calm tone and steady dealing speed |
| 4 | Recovery phase | 2m | Add deliberate correction, keep rhythm stable |
