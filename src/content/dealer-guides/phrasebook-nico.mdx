## 🗣️ Core Dealer Phrases

| Situation | Phrase | Notes | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening round | "Place your bets, please." | Even, neutral tone | 4-beat measure |
| End of betting | "No more bets, thank you." | Firm but calm | 4-beat |
| Dealing cards | "Good luck, everyone." | Warm tone | 2-beat |
| Checking blackjack | "Checking for blackjack…" | Slight pause before reveal | 6-beat |
| Paying out | "Paying even money." | Precise and paced | 3-beat |
| End of hand | "Thank you, good luck next round." | Fluid, steady rhythm | 8-beat |
